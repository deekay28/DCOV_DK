# NOTE on encryption at rest: no line here provides it, on purpose.
# sqlcipher3-binary used to be listed here, but it cannot actually encrypt
# anything as this app is architected: SQLAlchemy's SQLCipher support is a
# *synchronous* dialect (sqlite+pysqlcipher://), and aiosqlite - the async
# engine this app uses throughout - has no supported way to swap its
# underlying driver for SQLCipher's. Installing sqlcipher3-binary alongside
# aiosqlite would do nothing; leaving it in requirements.txt implied a
# protection that wasn't there. See docs/ADMIN_MANUAL.md's "Encryption at
# rest" section for what to actually do instead.
